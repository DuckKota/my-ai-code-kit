# Vocabulary reference

Words and phrases that spiked in published text after LLMs arrived. Kobak et al. (*Science Advances*, July 2025) measured the excess across 15M+ PubMed abstracts; Juzek & Ward (ACL 2025) traced the overuse partly to human-feedback training; Reinhart et al. (*PNAS*, 2025) and Russell et al. (ACL 2025) confirmed the same words in general prose. Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" keeps the curated list this file follows.

Rules of use:

- Replace, don't thesaurus. The fix for "leverage" is "use", not "utilize harness capitalize on".
- Take the list literally. Wikipedia's warning: a word being overused by AI does *not* mean its synonyms are. "Underscore" is on the list; "stress" is not.
- If the user's own text or the domain genuinely uses a word ("robust statistics", "the attack surface landscape"), keep it. Accuracy beats avoidance.
- A single hit is fine. Three in a paragraph is a signature.

## The list shifts by model generation

Wikipedia now dates the words. Use this to read *how old* a piece of AI text sounds and to know which words newer models have already stopped using (so their absence proves nothing).

| Era | Typical words |
|---|---|
| 2023 to mid-2024 (GPT-4) | additionally, boasts, bolstered, crucial, delve, emphasizing, enduring, garner, intricate, interplay, key, landscape, meticulous, pivotal, underscore, tapestry, testament, valuable, vibrant |
| Mid-2024 to mid-2025 (GPT-4o) | align with, bolstered, crucial, emphasizing, enhance, enduring, fostering, highlighting, pivotal, showcasing, underscore, vibrant |
| Mid-2025 onward (GPT-5) | emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing, plus the notability phrases below |

"Delve" was the famous 2023 tell and dropped off sharply in 2025. A 2026 draft full of "delve" is more likely a person imitating AI than AI.

Models also differ from each other. Wikipedia's notes: Grok overuses pseudo-scientific words (*causal*, *empirical*, *correlate*) and the "X rather than Y" construction, and still overuses *underscore*; ChatGPT and Grok dwell on "broader context" more than Gemini and Claude; Claude uses em dashes more than professional writers do, while ChatGPT (since GPT-5.1) suppresses them; ChatGPT and DeepSeek emit curly quotes, Gemini and Claude do not.

## Significance and legacy phrases

Wikipedia's "undue emphasis on significance" words to watch. Cut the clause; the fact before it is the sentence.

- a crucial / pivotal / vital / significant / key role or moment
- marking a pivotal moment, key turning point, setting the stage for
- underscores / highlights its importance or significance
- reflects broader trends, contributing to the broader
- symbolizing its ongoing / enduring / lasting
- enduring legacy, lasting legacy, indelible mark, deeply rooted
- evolving landscape, focal point
- generated debate, prompted broader reflection, raising philosophical questions, participated in public discussions

## Notability and coverage phrases (2025+)

Text that proves importance by describing the sources. Say what the source said, or cut.

- featured in / profiled in / cited in (followed by a list of outlets)
- independent coverage, significant coverage
- local / regional / national media outlets, trade publications, business or tech outlets
- written by a leading expert
- maintains an active social media presence, strong digital presence

## Vague connection phrases

Say the relationship instead: founded, member of, taught, caused by, used for.

- in connection with / to
- associated with (especially "widely / closely / particularly associated with")
- in association with
- connected with / to

## Chatbot phrases

Never inside a deliverable.

- Great question!, Of course!, Certainly!, You're absolutely right!
- I hope this helps, let me know, is there anything else, feel free to reach out
- Would you like me to..., want me to..., should I continue, more detailed breakdown, here is a...
- As of my last update, up to my last training update, as an AI
- While specific details are limited / scarce, not widely documented, based on available information, in the provided search results, maintains a low profile, keeps personal details private, likely [grew up / studied / began]

## Stock phrases

Cut these entirely; they carry no information.

- in today's fast-paced world / digital age / ever-changing landscape
- at the end of the day
- when it comes to
- take it to the next level
- in the realm of
- it's worth noting that / it's important to note that / it is crucial to remember
- needless to say
- a wide range of / a diverse array of
- best practices (unless naming specific ones)
- moving forward (as filler)
- last but not least, first and foremost
- let's dive in / let's explore / here's what you need to know
- X is the language of Y / the currency of Z (manufactured aphorisms)

## Wordiness (not an AI sign)

Trim for concision if the register wants it, but know that Wikipedia lists these among constructions *more common in human writing* than in AI output. Cutting them is editing, not humanizing, and the linter does not flag them.

- in order to → to
- due to the fact that → because
- the process of X-ing → X-ing
- as a result of → because of
- all of the → all the

## Puffery

Wikipedia's AI-cleanup project flags these as promotional tone regardless of who wrote them. Describe what the thing does, not how impressive it is.

- renowned, esteemed, acclaimed, celebrated
- rich cultural heritage, rich history, natural beauty, breathtaking
- nestled (in the heart of)
- stands as / serves as a beacon of
- world-class, premier, leading (unverifiable)
- award-winning (unless naming the award)
- commitment to, dedication to, exemplifies

## Connectives to ration

Not banned, but AI leans on them more than humans do, and Wikipedia lists sentence-initial "Additionally" as AI vocabulary outright. One or two per page is normal writing; the linter flags the third.

- Additionally / Furthermore / Moreover → often just start the next sentence
- However (sentence-initial, repeatedly) → vary or drop
- Overall / Ultimately / In essence → usually precedes an echo; cut the sentence
- Notably / Interestingly / Importantly → delete; the content shows it or it doesn't
